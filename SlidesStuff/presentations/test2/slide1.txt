Welcome to today's presentation on Pangea, Earth's ancient supercontinent. Imagine a time when all the continents we know today were joined together as one massive landmass. This supercontinent existed for nearly 120 million years and played a crucial role in shaping the Earth as we know it today. Join me as we explore the fascinating story of Pangea—its formation, its geography, the life it supported, its dramatic breakup, and the evidence that proves it once existed. Let's begin our journey back in time, nearly 300 million years.
